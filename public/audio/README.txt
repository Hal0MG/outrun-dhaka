Optional recordings. Put loopable files here and the game uses them instead of the generated sound:
  rain.ogg   steady rainfall on a street (about 20-40 s, seamless loop)
  city.ogg   Dhaka street ambience: distant traffic, voices, horns far off (30-60 s loop)
  river.ogg  river / lake lapping, boat engines far off (20-40 s loop)
.mp3 also works. Mono, 44.1 kHz, ~64-96 kbps is plenty (a few hundred KB each).
Use only CC0 or properly licensed recordings, and list them in CREDITS in the repo root.
